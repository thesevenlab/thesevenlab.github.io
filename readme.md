# Seven Lab

Welcome to the Software EVolution ENgineering (Seven) Lab at the [Department of Computer Science and Engineering](https://cse.iutoic-dhaka.edu/), [Islamic University of Technology](https://iutoic-dhaka.edu/) . The Seven Lab is co-led by Dr. Tajkia Rahman Toma and Dr. Mohayeminul Islam and focuses on advancing the field of software evolution through research, development, and collaboration.
